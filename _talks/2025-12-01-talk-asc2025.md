**Authors:** Pratik Nag, Andrew Zammit-Mangion, Noel Cressie, Sumeetpal Singh

[Presentation slides are available here](/files/Presentation_FNO-DST_ASC2025.pdf)

This talk presents the use of Fourier neural operators (FNOs) for constructing statistical dynamical spatio-temporal (DST) models for forecasting. An FNO is a flexible mapping of functions that approximates the solution operator of possibly unknown linear or nonlinear partial differential equations (PDEs) in a computationally efficient manner, so explicit knowledge of an underlying PDE is not required. Related paper: [Technometrics (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1080/00401706.2026.2736750).
